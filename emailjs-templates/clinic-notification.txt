EMAILJS TEMPLATE 1: clinic notification
Paste into EmailJS > Email Templates > Create New Template.

Settings panel (right-hand side of the template editor)
  To Email:    admin@eyehub.net.au            (add a second address comma-separated if reception wants a copy)
  From Name:   EyeHub Website
  From Email:  leave "Use Default Email Address" ticked
  Reply To:    {{email}}                      (so reception can hit Reply and it goes straight to the patient)

Subject
  New free assessment request: {{first_name}} {{last_name}}, {{preferred_datetime}}

Content (plain text is fine)
------------------------------------------------------------
A new FREE online laser vision suitability assessment has been requested from the Facebook landing page.

Name:            {{first_name}} {{last_name}}
Mobile:          {{phone}}
Email:           {{email}}

Preferred date:  {{preferred_date}}
Preferred time:  {{preferred_time}}

Wears:           {{about}}
Page tuned to:   {{tuned}}
Ticked on page:  {{pain_points}}

Ad / source:     {{lead_source}}
Submitted:       {{submitted_at}}
Page:            {{page_url}}

ACTION: book the time in the practice software and confirm with the patient by phone or SMS within one business day.
------------------------------------------------------------

Variables the page sends (case-sensitive):
  first_name, last_name, phone, email, preferred_date, preferred_time, preferred_datetime,
  about, tuned, pain_points, lead_source, page_url, submitted_at
