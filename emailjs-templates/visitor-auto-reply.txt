EMAILJS TEMPLATE 2 (optional): auto-reply to the visitor
Paste into EmailJS > Email Templates > Create New Template, then put its Template ID into
assets/js/config.js as autoReplyTemplateId.

Settings panel
  To Email:    {{email}}
  From Name:   EyeHub
  From Email:  leave "Use Default Email Address" ticked
  Reply To:    admin@eyehub.net.au

Subject
  We have your request, {{first_name}}. EyeHub free online assessment

Content
------------------------------------------------------------
Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks for requesting a free online laser vision suitability assessment with EyeHub.

Requested: {{preferred_date}} at {{preferred_time}} (Queensland time)

Our team will call or SMS you on {{phone}} within one business day to confirm the time and send the call details. If that time no longer suits, just reply to this email or call 07 5220 8990.

The assessment is free and there is no obligation. It is a preliminary screening only and does not replace an in-clinic examination.

EyeHub
Sunshine Coast and Noosa
eyehub.net.au
------------------------------------------------------------

Note: on the EmailJS free plan the auto-reply uses the second of the two template slots and one extra
request per booking (200 requests a month in total). The $9/month Personal plan removes both limits and adds
domain allow-listing.
